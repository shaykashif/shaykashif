Shay Kashif

Photographer · Filmmaker · Data Scientist

Hey, I'm Shay! I split my time between framing shots and tinkering with computers. Photography taught me to find signal in noise; data science gave me the tools to prove it.

Whether I'm behind a lens or inside a spreadsheet, I'm chasing the same thing: a clear, honest picture of how things really work, inside and out.

In summer 2025 I was a full-time analyst at Merton Capital Partners, researching affordable housing policy in the U.S. and South Florida. I've also built an interactive map of how redlining shaped Oakland and an off-grid LoRa radio network, and some of my writing has been published in The Exonian.
